**1. Coffee can of "rear" black 5/16 washer-head bolts, ~3/4" long, about 8. What uses them?**

The closest match in the files is 3846202, a hex bolt with a conical washer, 5/16-18 x 7/8, phosphate (black) finish (GM Standard Parts catalog (1989), p.136). **2. Door panel off without cracking the armrest: how many screws, where are the hidden ones?**
Per the 1968 service manual (p.1B-11): pull the window crank and lock-control clips with tool J-7797, take out the screws, then pry the panel off carefully at the plastic clips around its edge. The 1970 AIM (sheet K9) shows 4 screws 3919999 with chrome washers 140270 along the top (one pair at the front/middle, one pair at the rear upper corner) and 2 screws 9431733 near and below the pull handle. The AIM calls the armrest the PULL HANDLE ASM (chart 3863719, K9 item 7), and those 2 screws are the hidden ones. Clips 3932809 (with a screw 9425144) engage slots along the bottom edge. Before you lift the panel, take off the lock knob (wire retainer 4168122, K8 item 12) and the inside handle (screw 455698, 15-20 lb-in, K8 item 15). All panel screws are 10-15 lb-in. The 1968 manual says "4 screws at the inside corners"; the 1970 sheet's 4 top screws plus 2 at the handle win.
Grade: ANSWERED
Reason: the service manual gives the order and the clip warning; the AIM gives the screw count, locations and part numbers.

**3. Coupe rear window removable? What holds it in, what comes off first to save the stainless?**
Yes. The REAR WINDOW GLASS ASSEMBLY 3938098 (F18 item 11) is held by the rear window latch, LOCK ASM 3963103 (F19 item 2). Each latch is screwed up from below with screw 9418894 at 25-35 lb-in, and the window seals against weatherstrip 3964140 (F19 item 1). When it's out, it stows in tray 3972537 (F17). The stainless above the window is the MOLDING-RR ROOF UPPER 3972307-8 (F18 item 1), screwed to the body, so nothing on it has to come off: just release the latches and lift the window out. Per the 1968 manual (p.1B-46), the seal force is set by moving the locks and lower receiving plates in their slotted holes. To change the glass you drive out the frame dowel pins with a drift under 1/16" and re-caulk the frame.
Grade: ANSWERED
Reason: the AIM gives the parts and fasteners; the 1968 manual adds adjustment and glass service.

**8. What sealer does the AIM call out on the windshield frame (birdcage) before the glass goes in?**
Sheet F9 calls for seal 70a (GM 3926350, 134 in.) around the opening. Under it goes primer 76a (GM 9981282; 77a, GM 9981286, is the alternative) on the glass-to-frame bond line, and sealer 78b (GM 9985051, opt. 9981310) at the glass edge. The lower spacers are glued with 51m. The 1968 manual (p.1B-5) gives the procedure: pressure-sensitive sealing strip about 1/4" in from the glass edge, silane primer on the glass and frame, then one unbroken bead about 1/4" wide x 3/8" high. It uses kit 4226000, and the glass must overlap the pinchweld by at least 3/16". Water-test right away, and never aim a hard stream at soft caulk.
Grade: ANSWERED
Reason: the AIM has the material codes and GM numbers; the manual has the method.

**12. Headlight buckets: what holds the vacuum actuators on, and how many vacuum lines go where?**

**15. What holds the wiper door on and how is the vacuum actuator linked to it?**
The PANEL ASM-ACCESS DR 3942792 pivots on the control linkage arms with bolt 3922099, washer 3940537 and bushings 3922010 (D5). The 1968 manual counts 4 bolts. The actuator 5638605 is pinned to its body bracket with nut 271172 at 35-50 lb-in. Its rod runs through boot 3966577, and adjusting nut 3948074 (locked by jam nut 9417954) connects it to the linkage; set 3/8 + 1/8 in. with the piston bottomed and the door closed (D4).
