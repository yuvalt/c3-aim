# Restorer questions, results v2

Answers use only `dist/aim_sheets.md` and `dist/aim_parts.csv` (1970 AIM extract). What the files cover: UPC 0 general sheets (A2, A3, A12 spring chart, D1-D10 lube/cement/sealer/fill charts), UPC 1 F18 (coupe rear roof moldings), the full door section K1-K15, UPC 6 D2 (accelerator cable), UPC 12 D1 (LF engine-bay wiring diagram), option index A1, C07 A7, NA9 A5 (canceled), U69 A6 (ignition shield).

Grades: ANSWERED = fully from the files; PARTIAL = some of it from the files, the rest missing; NOT_COVERED = the files don't have it.

**2. Door panel off without cracking the armrest: how many screws, where are the hidden ones?**
Per sheet K9 (marked preliminary), the standard panel uses 4 screws 3919999 with washers 140270 across the top, a pair at front/middle and a pair at the rear upper corner (items 4, 3). There are also 2 screws 9431733 near and below the pull handle (item 6), and clips 3932809 in slots along the bottom with a screw 9425144 (items 5, 9). On the trim side you also have the inside handle screw 455698 (K8 item 15), the lock knob's wire retainer 4168122 (K8 item 12) and the crank escutcheon, which is held by 3 bent tabs (K5 note). The AIM gives no removal order and says nothing about the armrest cracking.
Grade: PARTIAL

**3. Coupe rear window removable? What holds it in, what comes off first to save the stainless?**
Sheet F18 lists the rear glass as 3938098 (item 11, replaced 3938097 on 10-14-69). It shows the stainless upper molding 3972307-8 (item 1) held by 2 screws 9431792 per side at 20-30 lb-in, driven up from under the roof panel through washers 3966796 (items 3, 2). The glass retention itself is on F17/F19, which I don't have, and the AIM never gives a removal order.
Grade: PARTIAL

**27. Door glass and run channel: what order, what holds the channel?**
On the coupe, sheet K12 has you put the glass and its parts in with all bolts loose (step 3). You then set the glass to the windshield-side weatherstrip and tighten the front guide cam upper bolts "B" and idler cam bolts "C" (step 6). The rear cam is set to gage blocks and you tighten "G", then "B", then the rear anti-rattle bolt "D" (step 9), and you recheck after body drop. The guide cams (the channels) use screw 3966667 at 50-75 lb-in, top and bottom, with plate 3966666 at the front top. The rear cam's lower stop uses screw 9418970 (sheet K4, items 2, 5, 6).
Grade: ANSWERED

**28. Outside door release button: how many nuts, gasket underneath?**
