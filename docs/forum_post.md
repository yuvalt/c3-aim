Title: Asking ChatGPT questions about the 1970 AIM — useful?

Restoring a '70 coupe, and I got tired of flipping through the AIM for one part number. So I turned my AIM into text that ChatGPT or Claude can search: part numbers, torques, sealers, and what bolts to what. Only the doors and a few other sheets so far. It starts with 1970 but works for any year's AIM. It shares facts only, with no scans or drawings, and always gives the sheet so you can check your own manual. 

Would you use it? Did anyone build something like that already? What would you ask it first?  

Here's some examples of what is can answer currently:

**Q: Found part 9431792 in a bag. What is it?**
Sheet F18, item 3. Screw that holds the rear roof upper molding (3972307-8) to the roof panel, 2 per side, 20–30 in-lb. It goes up from under the panel through washer 3966796.

**Q: Any adhesive under that molding?**
Sealing strip 3851624 under it (D7 calls it plastic seam cement). Plus 1.02 oz of 51n adhesive where the molding meets the weatherstrip, and a 3/8" bead of 55d black sealer at the joint (codes from sheets D5/D6).

**Q: Which sheet shows the door hinge screws?**
K6. Screw 3918503 (item 4), 25–30 ft-lb, at the hinge pillar and the door.

**Q: Are the door weatherstrips L/R specific?**
Yes. K3 item 9: 3966561-2 is a left/right pair. Convertibles use 3966569-70 instead.

**Q: Outside door handle: what holds it, and is there a gasket?**
Yes. K7: the gasket is item 11, 3956022 (the AIM calls it SEAL), between the handle 9718410-1 and the door skin. Handle is held by nuts 3866846 at 35–50 in-lb. Don't mix it up with item 8 GASKET 4587931, which is for the key lock cylinder.

**Q: What holds the molding on top of the door?**
The AIM just calls it MOULDING (K3 item 1, 3954525-6), so I'm assuming that's the one. Screws 476784 and 9418719, 10–15 in-lb.

**Q: What bolts the accelerator cable bracket, and what replaced 3972625?**
D2: bolt 9419000, 75–95 in-lb. Revision 5-19-70: bracket changed from 3972625 to 3992386.

**Q: All the fasteners in the door?**
About 40 across K1–K15, each with sheet, torque and what it holds. A few: hinge screws 3918503 at 25–30 ft-lb (K6), striker 7660559 at 35–50 ft-lb (K3), regulator screws 3856691 at 50–75 in-lb (K5). That last one is 1/4-20 x 5/8 hex with captured washer, per vendor catalogs (not the AIM).

**Q: What holds the front bumper on?**
Not covered yet. Bumpers are UPC 14, and I haven't done those sheets.

**Q: What size is 9431792?**
Unknown. The AIM only gives GM numbers, never sizes. Working on increasing the number of fastener I know.

That last one is the big gap. I'm working on sizes now. If anyone has the GM Standard Parts Catalog, that would solve most of it.
