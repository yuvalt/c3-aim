### Q1
**Q:** Got a coffee can of bolts from the PO marked "rear." Black 5/16 bolts with washer heads, about 3/4" long, maybe 8 of them. What on a 70 coupe uses those?

**A:** The AIM never gives fastener sizes, so I can't match bolts by 5/16 x 3/4 dimensions from the manual. The only "rear" fasteners in the extracted pages are the rear roof upper molding screws 9431792 (20-30 lb-in, 2 per side) and plate/guide screws 9428715 (10-15 lb-in) on 1970 AIM UPC 1 sheet F18, items 3-4. Their low inch-pound torques suggest small trim screws, not 5/16 bolts, but that is inference. The rear bumper, rear suspension, body mount and rear-compartment sheets are not extracted yet, so I can't say what uses them.

**Grade:** NOT_IN_AIM  
**Reason:** Identifying a part from its bolt size isn't possible with the AIM, which gives no sizes; the answer says so and doesn't guess.

### Q2

General knowledge: work the bottom clips loose with a flat trim tool after all screws are out, and don't pry on the pull handle.

**Grade:** ANSWERED  
**Reason:** K9/K5/K8 fasteners cited correctly; counts are given as drawn, the missing order and the PRELIMINARY status are flagged, and general knowledge is labeled.

**Grade:** ANSWERED  
**Reason:** K4 cams, K2 lift-channel stack and the K12 step sequence are correct, and the answer correctly says K12 lettered bolts have no numeric torque; it skips screws F (step 15) in the order summary.

**Top 3 format problems (not coverage)**
1. **The "How it's fastened" lines and the fastener index lose or misattach variants and torques.** K3 screw 9419858 and adhesive 51c are shown holding only the *convertible* weatherstrip 3966569-70, so a coupe owner never sees 3966561-2 connected to its fasteners. The section-K index gives 3856691 at K8 as "25-35 lb-in → lock control, remote control", but the K8 body says the remote control screw is 35-50. The CSV keeps only one torque per row (25-35). Counts written as "(1)" read like torque triangle 1 (K4 "(1) at 50-75 lb-in"). Several lines have an empty subject ("-  glues …", "-  holds …").
2. **No glossary from AIM terms to restorer terms, and no "Ref." pointers to related sheets.** "Side glass w/strip" is the door-glass rear seal, "guide cam" is the run channel, "remote control" is the inside handle, and "roof panel" is the T-top. F18 doesn't point to F17/F19 (rear window storage/locks). This mapping gap is what produced the Q31 error. It also makes every not-covered answer weaker, because the assistant can't name the right unextracted sheet (registry titles would fix that if shipped as a sheet index).
3. **LH/RH pairs are only searchable by the first number.** Pairs are stored only as "3966561-2", so grepping the RH number from a parts bag (3966562, 3972308) returns nothing in either file; the registry splits them. Pair labeling is also inconsistent: 9718410-1 handle and 9718280-1 lock carry no "(LH/RH)" label. Related CSV problems: `name_expanded` is overloaded (on material rows it packs "code · usage · upc · subgrp"), there are no qty/attaches-to/variant columns, and 3851624 is "SEALING STRIP" on F18 but "CEMENT-PLASTIC SEAM" on D7 with no flag.
